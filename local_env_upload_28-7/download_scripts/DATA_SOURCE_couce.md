# Couce et al. (2020) — North Sea trawling effort

## Source

- **Dataset:** Reconstruction of North Sea trawling effort 1985–2015 (version 2)
- **Authors:** Couce, Engelhard, Schratzberger (2019)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.14466/CefasDataHub.61
- **Cefas Data Hub holding ID:** 19722
- **Recordset ID:** 10953
- **Downloaded:** 2026-07-05 via Cefas Data API (`/api/export/10953?format=csv`)

## Local file

`NorthSea_trawling_effort_1985to2015_REVIEW_v2.csv`

## Structure

Annual fishing hours per ICES rectangle (`ICES_rect`), split by gear (Otter, Beam) and data type (Compiled, Estimated, Reconstructed, Estimation_Error). H2 uses **Reconstructed** hours only; total pressure = otter + beam reconstructed hours per rectangle-year, then mean annual hours per rectangle over 1985–2015.

## Citation

Couce, E., Engelhard, G. H., & Schratzberger, M. (2020). Reconstructing three decades of total international trawling effort in the North Sea. *Earth System Science Data*, 12, 373–410. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-12-373-2020
